How this plan works

01
Start with a class you like

Pick the class you will actually play. The suggestion below follows that choice.

02
Learn the six crafting professions

Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Leatherworking, and Tailoring each give a point at skill 150, 225, and 300. Herbalism, Mining, and Skinning do not.

03
Do not gather the same thing

Stay the same faction if you want to mail items or play together. Give the two characters you play at the same time different gathering skills.
