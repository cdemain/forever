How this plan works

01
Start with a role you like

Pick Tank, Melee DPS, Ranged DPS, or Healing. Suggestions follow that preference.

02
Learn the six crafting professions

Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Leatherworking, and Tailoring each grant points at skill 150, 225, and 300.

03
Do not gather the same thing

Stay the same faction to mail items or play together. Give characters you play at the same time different gathering professions.
